# RAGA NIDHI

There are three types of Bageshree according to number of swaras used in Aroha and Avroha.

In the Audhava-Shadhava type Ri and Pa are omitted in Aroha, and Pa only in Avroha. Its Aroha and Avroha are as under:-

This type of Bageshree is called Shuddha Bageshree.

The third type is the Shadhava-Sampoorna type. The vivja swara in Aroha is Pa. Its Aroha and Avroha are:

Avroha

Pa is used in a vakra fashion as swara and not straight or saral as swara.

Some use Tiwa Ni when halting on Tara Shadja, as ri and so on. This sounds nice and there is no harm done to the ragabhasa. But use of Tiwara Ni is not common.

In all the types of Bageshree the vadi swara is Ma and Avroha is Ga. Popular compositions in Bageshree are:

1 Mohé Manava – Vilambit Khyal
2 Kaun Gar Bhai – Drut Kinay- Keeni

# BAGESHREE

Raga Bahar of Hindustani Sangeet is allied to Bageshree.

In Bahar both Nishads are used. Pa is taken as a common note and not used in Avroha in some contexts. Swara Dh is not used in Avroha. See Bahar for details.

Raga Abhog or Abhog Kanad also resembles Bageshree, but in Abhog Ni is completely dropped, and Ga is used in vakra fashion occasionally to remove the chaya of Bageshree created by the straight use of Pa. See Bahar.

Karnatak Sangeet

There is no Raga called Bageshree in Karnatak Sangeet. Vegeeshwari of Karnatak Sangeet uses the Shuddha swaras of Karnatak Sangeet. It is a janya of No. 1 Melakarta Kanakangi. Shuddha swaras of Karnatak Sangeet are different from those of Hindustani Sangeet. Hence Vegeeshwari of Karnatak Sangeet has no resemblance to Bageshree in the Hindustani sense.
